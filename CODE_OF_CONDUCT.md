# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical
expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Protect privacy and never request credentials or customer information.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
other project-related spaces.
